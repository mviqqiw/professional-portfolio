
# Sales & Leasing Brokerage Job Simulation

## Overview

Completed a Commercial Real Estate Job Simulation with CBRE through Forage, gaining practical experience in retail leasing analysis, tenant selection, market research, and investment sales marketing.

## Key Areas

- Commercial Real Estate Leasing
- Retail Rent Roll Analysis
- Tenant Selection and Evaluation
- Market and Demographic Research
- Investment Sales
- Offering Memorandum Preparation

## Key Projects

- Analyzed the Retail Rent Roll for San Miguel Shopping Center in Phoenix, Arizona, identifying vacant retail suites and evaluating prospective tenants based on space requirements, business categories, and local market demand.
- Developed tenant recommendations and leasing strategies to improve the shopping center's tenant mix while avoiding unnecessary business duplication.
- Researched the Dallas–Fort Worth market and Frisco submarket for The Edison at Frisco Apartments.
- Prepared a location overview covering demographic profiles, apartment market statistics, and property location maps for an investment sales Offering Memorandum.
