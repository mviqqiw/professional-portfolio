
# Product Management Job Simulation

## Overview

Completed a Product Management Job Simulation focused on analyzing player behavior, evaluating product performance, and preparing a stakeholder presentation for Legends of Aether, a free-to-play mobile strategy RPG.

The simulation involved identifying relevant KPIs, prioritizing player insights, and organizing a cross-functional project plan to support data-driven product decisions.

## Key Areas

- Product Performance Analysis
- KPI Identification and Recommendation
- Player Engagement and Retention
- Churn and Conversion Analysis
- Player Insight Prioritization
- Stakeholder Presentation Planning
- Cross-functional Collaboration
