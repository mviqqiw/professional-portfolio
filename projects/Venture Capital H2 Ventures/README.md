
# Venture Capital Job Simulation

## Overview

Completed a Venture Capital Job Simulation with H2 Ventures through Forage, gaining practical experience in evaluating early-stage startups, assessing investment opportunities, performing startup valuations, and developing creative growth strategies.

## Key Areas

- Venture Capital Fundamentals
- Investment Selection and Evaluation
- Startup Valuation using the Venture Capital Method
- Startup Growth Strategies

## Key Projects

- Identified and evaluated 10 promising early-stage startups with potential investment opportunities.
- Developed a weighted investment selection grading framework to assess startup investment potential.
- Conducted a startup valuation using the Venture Capital Method, including financial assumptions, terminal value, and expected investor returns.
- Proposed three creative customer acquisition and growth strategies for Stockspot, an H2 Ventures portfolio company.

## Skills Developed

- Venture Capital Analysis
- Financial Modelling
- Startup Valuation
- Investment Research
- Market Analysis
- Strategic Thinking
- Growth Strategy Development
- Excel Spreadsheet Analysis
